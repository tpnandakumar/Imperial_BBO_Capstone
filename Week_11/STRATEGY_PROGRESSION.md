# Strategy Progression Through Week 11

## Purpose

This document records how the optimisation approach developed by the end of Week 11. It focuses on changes in analytical practice rather than simply listing weekly results.

## Early stage

The first rounds were dominated by broad exploration. With little information about the hidden functions, the main aim was to establish baseline behaviour and identify whether any regions were clearly productive or clearly weak.

## Function specific refinement

As more observations became available, the eight functions stopped being treated in the same way. Strong regions were refined, weak regions were explored more broadly, and repeated failures were used to redirect the search.

## Historical trajectory analysis

By the middle rounds, the full sequence of previous queries became more useful than the latest result alone. Earlier best points, local movement and the direction of successful and unsuccessful changes began to shape the next submission.

## Clustering and regional recovery

Week 10 introduced a stronger regional view. Queries were considered in relation to nearby historical points and recurring high performing areas. This led to a Week 11 submission that combined exact repeats, local refinement and recovery towards earlier stronger regions.

The Week 11 outcomes supported that approach. Every function improved relative to Week 10. Functions 2 and 5 reached new verified best values, while Functions 1 and 8 reproduced earlier best values exactly.

## PCA and structural comparison

After the Week 11 results, the accumulated query histories were examined using principal component analysis for Functions 3 to 8. The aim was to see whether the search trajectories had become concentrated in a smaller number of directions.

The analysis found strong concentration, but this was not treated as proof that the hidden objective surfaces were effectively one or two dimensional. PCA described the geometry of the submitted queries, and those queries had already been shaped by earlier strategy choices.

The Week 12 decision therefore compared PCA structure with direct objective evidence. Where both agreed, as in Function 5, the structural evidence strengthened the case for a further controlled move. Where the historical best was more informative, the direct performance evidence was retained.

## Strategic growth

The main progression by Week 11 can be summarised as:

```text
Broad exploration
        |
        v
Function specific refinement
        |
        v
Historical trajectory comparison
        |
        v
Regional clustering and recovery
        |
        v
PCA structural analysis
        |
        v
Method comparison
        |
        v
Function specific Week 12 strategy
```

The key development is not simply the addition of more techniques. It is the move towards comparing methods and keeping the one that is better supported by the evidence for each function.
