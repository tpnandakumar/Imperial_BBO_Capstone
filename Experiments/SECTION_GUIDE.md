# Experiments

This directory contains verified experimental records that support the Imperial Bayesian Black Box Optimisation Capstone.

## Recorded experiments

### Week 06 validation

Records the Week 06 submission, returned outputs and exact changes from Week 05.

### Week 07 validation

Records the Week 07 recovery experiment, including the major improvements in Functions 3, 4, 5 and 6.

### Week 08 validation

Records the Week 08 local refinement experiment and the improvements in Functions 2, 5 and 7.

### Week 09 validation

Records the Week 09 refinement experiment, including the new Function 5 best and the resulting strategy classification.

### Week 10 validation

Records the Week 10 stability and refinement experiment that provided the evidence used to develop the Strategic Optimisation Engine.

### SOE Experiment 001: Week 11 validation

Evaluates the Strategic Optimisation Engine, version 1.0, against the verified Week 10 baseline. The engine used the complete Weeks 1 to 10 history to select the Week 11 submission. The returned Week 11 outputs improved all eight functions relative to Week 10.

Only verified source inputs, returned outputs, exact Decimal comparisons and existing code are included. The experiment records supplement the weekly folders and do not replace them.
