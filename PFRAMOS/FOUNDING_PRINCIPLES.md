# PFRAMOS Founding Principles

## 1. Open auditable-box engine

No optimisation decision is accepted unless its evidence path can be reconstructed from the original observations through every active node to the final recommendation.

## 2. Every node is an optimisation node

Each node has a declared local objective and must demonstrate how its output contributes to the global decision.

## 3. Equal-opportunity emergent terminality

Every eligible node may become the temporary Terminal Active Node. Terminality is not predetermined and is not assigned by uncontrolled randomness. It emerges from relevance, evidence strength, robustness, independent support and cross-node validation.

## 4. Dynamic active pathways

Inactive nodes remain dormant. Active nodes form the problem-specific pathway through feed-forward, feedback, lateral and non-adjacent bidirectional multi-spoke links.

## 5. Strength-aware integration

Signals retain separate measures of magnitude, confidence, stability, identifiability, independence, fragility and evidence lineage. Correlated evidence is not counted as independent confirmation.

## 6. Fit regulation

Model complexity must be proportionate to evidence. Walk-forward prediction, stability and robustness determine whether a model is underfitting, appropriately fitted or overfitting.

## 7. Dynamic node evolution

Nodes may progress through proposed, experimental, validated, eligible, active, dormant, deprecated, retired and archived states. Admission and retirement depend on demonstrated utility, independence, robustness and auditability.

## 8. PIMF compatibility

PIMF diagnoses influence and trajectory. PFRAMOS converts validated evidence into optimisation action. Neither framework rewrites or invalidates the other.

## 9. Historical integrity

Past BBO submissions remain unchanged. Retrospective analyses are explicitly labelled as counterfactual and never presented as observed outcomes.

## 10. Live-submission safeguard

No experimental node or candidate controls a live BBO submission unless it survives comparison with the existing strategy, walk-forward testing, perturbation testing and full audit review.
