# PFRAMOS Emergent Route Discovery

## Principle

Emergent behaviour may be more than an unexpected output. It may indicate a latent capability, a hidden coupling between nodes, or a more efficient conduit that has not yet been explicitly represented.

PFRAMOS therefore treats useful emergence as topological evidence.

## Core sequence

```text
Unexpected behaviour
        ↓
Trace contributing nodes and vectors
        ↓
Separate signal, bias and noise
        ↓
Test compatible emergent combinations
        ↓
Reconstruct hidden coupling
        ↓
Build candidate conduit
        ↓
Laminarise and compress routing
        ↓
Validate quality, robustness and efficiency
```

## Emergent combination classes

- additive
- synergistic
- gating
- stabilising
- corrective
- routing
- catalytic
- translational

Each compound behaviour is treated as a new candidate with complete lineage.

## Pisharam Emergent Behaviour Synthesis

Pisharam Emergent Behaviour Synthesis, or PEBS, evaluates whether two or more partial, weak or unstable behaviours can combine to create a stronger capability or a lower-friction route.

The system records:

- contributing behaviour identifiers
- active nodes and conduits
- vector direction and magnitude
- compatibility
- synergy
- stabilisation
- novelty
- routing gain
- residual risk
- compound capability score

## Pisharam Bias Vector Modulation

Bias is treated as a directional vector rather than only as a binary defect.

Available actions are:

- suppress
- counterbalance
- redirect
- repurpose conditionally
- observe

Productive inductive bias may be retained when it improves validated performance without producing unacceptable harm or distortion.

## Controlled unpredictability

Unpredictability may support exploration, route discovery and escape from local optima. PFRAMOS assigns a bounded exploration-randomness budget based on:

- uncertainty
- stagnation
- nodal disagreement
- novelty deficit
- risk
- proximity to a final decision

Randomness is reduced as risk or decision proximity increases. Final decisions must use a reproducible deterministic pathway.

## Pisharam Vector Conduiting

Every behaviour vector carries:

- magnitude
- direction
- confidence
- coherence
- persistence
- risk
- compute cost
- variance

PFRAMOS may amplify, attenuate, rotate, split, merge, reverse or divert the vector. The aim is to direct valid power through the strongest coherent conduit.

## Laminar conduit criteria

A candidate route should reduce:

- routing conflict
- redundant transitions
- variance
- compute cost
- nodal congestion

It should preserve or improve:

- validated quality
- coherence
- robustness
- reproducibility
- uncertainty control

## Scientific safeguard

No emergent route is promoted because it appears novel or powerful. It must pass retrospective testing, isolated perturbation testing and prospective shadow validation.

## Governing statement

Wrongly directed power is a challenge. Coherently directed power is strength. Emergent behaviour may reveal where that power can flow more effectively.
