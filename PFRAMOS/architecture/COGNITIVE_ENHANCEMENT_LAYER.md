# PFRAMOS Cognitive Enhancement Layer

## Purpose

The Cognitive Enhancement Layer augments the quality of analysis and decision support without replacing human judgement.

It strengthens:

- selective attention
- working-memory management
- evidence prioritisation
- alternative-hypothesis generation
- counterfactual reasoning
- contradiction detection
- confidence calibration
- metacognitive checking
- retrieval of missing evidence

## Operating sequence

```text
Task state
    ↓
Attention and memory-load assessment
    ↓
Evidence-quality and conflict assessment
    ↓
Hypothesis generation
    ↓
Counterfactual testing
    ↓
Contradiction scan
    ↓
Confidence calibration
    ↓
PIMF influence diagnosis
    ↓
PFRAMOS conduit selection
    ↓
Human-reviewed decision
```

## Relationship with sequence intelligence

The unified sequence-intelligence layer supplies:

- persistent rules
- short-term context
- selective long-range state
- surprise-sensitive long-term memory
- adaptive forgetting
- quarantine memory

The Cognitive Enhancement Layer decides how these resources should support a task.

## Relationship with PIMF

PIMF examines how evidence and influence develop across observations and higher-order delta space. It identifies emerging, dominant, reversing, oscillating and boundary influences.

The Cognitive Enhancement Layer uses this diagnosis to determine whether to widen hypothesis search, increase verification, retrieve further evidence or defer a decision.

## Relationship with the BBO engine

For BBO, the layer may:

- focus analysis on the most influential dimensions
- compress historical evidence into task-specific working memory
- generate competing candidate explanations
- test counterfactual input movements
- identify contradictions between local and historical trends
- calibrate confidence before candidate selection

It must not expose private candidate coordinates or update live submissions without approval.

## Human control

Human review is mandatory when:

- risk is high
- uncertainty remains high
- evidence quality is weak
- contradictions remain unresolved
- a live BBO submission is involved
- publication claims are being finalised

## Scientific requirement

Cognitive enhancement is a capability claim only after controlled testing demonstrates improved accuracy, calibration, robustness, efficiency or decision quality. Architectural intent alone is not proof of enhancement.
