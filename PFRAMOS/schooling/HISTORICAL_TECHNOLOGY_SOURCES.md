# Historical Technology Schooling Sources

## Purpose

Previously tested technologies should not be discarded after an initial review. Their papers, repositories, figures, derived analyses and validation outcomes form a reusable historical evidence corpus.

The initial registered sources are:

- HEBO
- Netflix technical evidence
- NVIDIA technical evidence

## Source roles

### HEBO

Used for:

- Bayesian optimisation comparison
- heterogeneous input handling
- exploration and exploitation strategy
- surrogate and acquisition analysis
- PFRAMOS optimisation-node development

### Netflix

Used for:

- recommendation systems
- large-scale machine-learning operations
- ranking and personalisation concepts
- representation, selection and feedback-loop bias
- efficient production experimentation

Only official publications and explicitly permitted public data may be used. Private subscriber or behavioural data are excluded.

### NVIDIA

Used for:

- accelerated computing
- distributed and parallel training
- model efficiency
- hardware-aware routing
- synthetic data and simulation concepts
- compute and energy measurement

Hardware-specific claims must be separated from generalisable methods.

## Training status

These sources are approved for schooling review, but not for indiscriminate direct training. Each item must be converted into a versioned evidence record and must pass:

- source verification
- licence review
- version or commit pinning
- deduplication
- contamination analysis
- relevance scoring
- independent reproduction where feasible

## Synthesis

After independent revalidation, compatible elements may be combined with BBO-derived evidence and P-C4. Any materially transformed method must retain its original lineage and may be classified as Pisharamised only after demonstrable redesign and validation.
