# PFRAMOS Paper Series

## Publication principle

PFRAMOS will be published as a connected series of focused papers. Each paper must answer one principal research question, use its own evidence package and remain independently understandable.

The series avoids cramming architecture, optimisation, concurrent schooling, bias modulation, emergent behaviour and publishing applications into one manuscript.

## Shared evidence rule

The same experiment may support more than one paper only when each manuscript answers a distinct question. Shared datasets and infrastructure must be declared. Text, figures and analyses must not be duplicated in a way that constitutes redundant publication.
