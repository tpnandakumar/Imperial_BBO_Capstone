# PFRAMOS Scientific Narrative Arc

## Governing principle

The PFRAMOS publication programme should read as one coherent scientific story told through multiple independently publishable papers.

Each paper acts like a chapter. It must have its own question, methods, results and conclusion, but it should also move the wider PFRAMOS story forward.

## Story structure

### Chapter 1: The problem

Sparse sequential optimisation is difficult because evidence is limited, uncertainty is high and a single optimiser may fit some regions while failing in others.

**Paper 1** introduces the core PFRAMOS architecture and answers:

> Can multiple optimisation nodes be regulated, compared and routed through a coherent auditable system?

### Chapter 2: The cost of thinking

A stronger system is not enough if it consumes unnecessary compute, memory or energy.

**Paper 2** introduces P-C4 and answers:

> Can quality be preserved or improved while computation is allocated only where it has the greatest validated value?

### Chapter 3: How the system learns

A static framework cannot remain effective when new data, tasks and evidence continue to arrive.

**Paper 3** develops concurrent multi-source schooling and answers:

> Can multiple isolated learning lanes improve the system without contaminating each other or causing catastrophic forgetting?

### Chapter 4: When direction becomes distortion

Learning systems inherit and develop directional tendencies. Some are harmful, while others may be useful inductive structures.

**Paper 4** develops bias-vector modulation and answers:

> Can bias be detected, measured, redirected or repurposed without concealing harm?

### Chapter 5: When the unexpected becomes a route

Unexpected behaviour may not only be noise. It may reveal latent coupling, an unexplored capability or a more efficient path.

**Paper 5** develops emergent behaviour synthesis and answers:

> Can compatible emergent behaviours be combined into stable, validated laminar conduits?

### Chapter 6: Turning weak candidates into strong nodes

External methods may be incomplete, inefficient or poorly matched to the system. Some can still be transformed.

**Paper 6** develops technology mouldability and Pisharamisation and answers:

> When should a candidate technology be rejected, retained, modulated or transformed into a new validated node?

### Chapter 7: Different data, different learning

Not all evidence should be processed in the same way. Papers, code, time series, tables, simulations and multimodal data support different forms of learning.

**Paper 7** develops the data-form learning router and answers:

> Does learning improve when the method is matched to the structure and risks of the data?

### Chapter 8: Application to publishing intelligence

The framework is then applied to a complex real-world domain that combines language, structure, metadata, bias, continuity and human judgement.

**Paper 8** applies PFRAMOS to Honeycomb Publications and answers:

> Can auditable multi-source learning improve publishing and manuscript intelligence without losing human control?

## Narrative continuity rule

Every paper should include a brief section titled:

## Position in the PFRAMOS Series

That section must explain:

- what the previous paper established
- what gap remains
- what this paper contributes
- what the next paper will examine

## Standalone publication rule

Although the papers form a story, each must remain independently understandable. No essential method, result or definition may be hidden in another paper.

## Repetition control

The core PFRAMOS identity may be summarised briefly in each paper, but the full architecture should be described only in Paper 1. Later papers should cite Paper 1 or the versioned technical report.

## Final objective

The complete series should show not merely that PFRAMOS exists, but how it develops from an optimisation architecture into an adaptive, auditable and efficient learning system.
