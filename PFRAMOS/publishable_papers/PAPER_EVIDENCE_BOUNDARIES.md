# Paper Evidence Boundaries

## Purpose

Each paper in the PFRAMOS series must make one principal contribution and use a defined evidence package. This prevents overloading, duplicated claims and fragmented reporting of the same experiment.

## Shared infrastructure

The following may be described briefly across multiple papers:

- PFRAMOS system identity
- append-only training logs
- dataset manifests
- protected-test policy
- reproducibility requirements
- P-C4 resource reporting

Each manuscript should cite the primary architecture paper rather than repeat the full architecture.

## Paper-specific boundaries

### Paper 1

Owns the formal core architecture, optimisation performance and architecture ablations.

### Paper 2

Owns compute, memory, energy, scheduling and heterogeneous-resource claims.

### Paper 3

Owns concurrent schooling, lane isolation, cross-lane synthesis and forgetting analysis.

### Paper 4

### Paper 5

### Paper 6

### Paper 7

### Paper 8

Owns the publishing application, Honeycomb Publications evidence and human review.

## Reuse control

- A numerical result must have one primary manuscript owner.
- Secondary manuscripts may reference the result but must cite the primary paper or clearly state that the analysis is reused.
- Figures must not be duplicated without a justified purpose and clear attribution.
- A dataset can support several papers, but each paper must use a distinct research question and analysis plan.
- Negative findings should be assigned to the paper whose hypothesis they test.
- Supplementary material must not be used to conceal essential methodology.

## Publication sequence safeguard

Later papers may be developed concurrently, but they must not assume that an earlier paper has been accepted. Until publication, shared foundational material should cite a versioned preprint or technical report where permitted.
