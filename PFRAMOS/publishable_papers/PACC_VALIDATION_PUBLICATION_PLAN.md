# PACC Validation and Publication Pipeline

## Purpose

Open cognitive datasets discovered by the Scout programme must support both scientific validation and paper development. The same evidence must not be duplicated across papers without a defined primary owner.

## Pipeline

```text
Open-source dataset discovery
        ↓
Licence, provenance and privacy review
        ↓
Cognitive-domain mapping
        ↓
Data-form learning route
        ↓
Mathematical model validation
        ↓
Protected testing
        ↓
PIMF influence analysis
        ↓
PFRAMOS regulatory decision
        ↓
Training-result record
        ↓
Automatic PACC paper assignment
```

## PACC paper tracks

1. Foundations and mathematical cognitive state model
2. Computational memory circuit
3. Computational semantic cognition
4. Computational visuospatial cognition
5. Executive control, abstraction and cognitive flexibility
6. Social, behavioural and metacognitive cognition
7. Acquired cognitive development through schooling
8. Integrated functional cognitive network validation

## Evidence ownership

- Attention, working memory, episodic memory and retrieval evidence primarily belongs to Paper 2.
- Semantic memory, naming, language and fluency evidence primarily belongs to Paper 3.
- Visuospatial cognition, praxis and calculation evidence primarily belongs to Paper 4.
- Executive control, abstraction and contextual orientation evidence primarily belongs to Paper 5.
- Social cognition, emotion, behaviour, motivation and metacognition evidence primarily belongs to Paper 6.
- Cross-domain network experiments primarily belong to Paper 8.

## Publication gate

A cognitive-network result cannot enter a manuscript as measured evidence unless it has:

- a dataset manifest
- a fixed mathematical model version
- a protected test
- reproducible outputs
- PIMF analysis
- a PFRAMOS decision record
- privacy and licence clearance
- no unsupported clinical claim

## Clinical boundary

Open datasets may validate computational cognitive functions. They do not by themselves validate a clinical diagnostic instrument or establish human clinical equivalence.

Any clinical claim requires separate governance, appropriate patient data, ethical approval, clinical validation and human expert oversight.
