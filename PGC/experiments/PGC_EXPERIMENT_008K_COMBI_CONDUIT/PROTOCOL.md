# PGC Experiment 008K: Combi Conduit

## Status

Protocol locked for execution.

## Governing principle

The anchor is no longer a single conduit with an attached capture layer. It is a combined conduit in which Braided ZMI and Fish-Net ZMI operate as one coordinated protected system.

## Core architecture

```text
Outer exploratory field
        ↓
Fish-Net ZMI capture lattice
        ↓
Relaminarisation layer
        ↓
Braided ZMI axial core
        ↓
Combined protected anchor
```

## Protected states

Primary protected anchor:

`p_combi(x) = Combine(FishNetZMI(x), BraidedZMI(x))`

Internal rollback state:

`p_rollback(x) = BraidedZMI(x)`

The Fish-Net component captures persistent and locally influential evidence. The Braided ZMI component preserves stable local competence weighting and axial decision integrity.

## Combination rule

The final anchor probability is:

`p_combi(x) = alpha(x) p_fishnet(x) + [1 - alpha(x)] p_braided(x)`

where `alpha(x)` is determined by:

- local Zone of Maximum Influence strength
- capture persistence
- model agreement
- calibration quality
- predicted rescue probability
- predicted harm probability

## Relaminarisation requirement

No outer ripple, bubble, LLM or emergent route may enter the combined anchor directly.

All external evidence must pass through:

1. persistence testing
2. ZMI knot reinforcement
3. turbulence damping
4. calibration check
5. rescue-minus-harm gate

## Dynamic behaviour

The Combi Conduit may:

- widen for exploration
- tighten the Fish-Net aperture
- strengthen selected ZMI knots
- recruit locally competent cores
- close weak peripheral strands
- reopen specialist paths
- relaminarise before axial entry
- roll back to bare Braided ZMI

## Experiment arms

1. Braided ZMI core
2. Fish-Net ZMI capture
3. Fixed-weight Combi Conduit
4. Validation-weighted Combi Conduit
5. Case-adaptive Combi Conduit
6. Combi Conduit with relaminarisation
7. Complete Combi Conduit with rollback

## Validation design

- identical datasets, seeds and splits to Experiment 008J
- all combination weights selected on validation data only
- protected-test labels excluded from tuning
- paired case-level comparison against Braided ZMI and Fish-Net ZMI
- report accuracy, macro-F1, balanced accuracy, log loss, rescues, harms, net rescue, intervention rate and worst-seed accuracy

## Promotion criteria

The Combi Conduit is promoted only if it:

- exceeds Fish-Net ZMI mean protected-test accuracy
- preserves or improves worst-case accuracy
- produces positive net rescue
- causes no material increase in harms
- improves or preserves calibration
- repeats its gain across datasets and seeds

## Locked interpretation

Braided ZMI provides the protected axial decision structure. Fish-Net ZMI provides distributed capture and local knot reinforcement. Together they form one combined anchor rather than a core-plus-wrapper arrangement.
