# Graphs and Infographics

## Summary

The final visuals show that progress differed markedly across the eight functions. Function 5 followed the clearest sustained upward trajectory, while Functions 3 and 6 improved in the final round. Several other functions were best represented by retention or recovery of earlier strong points. The normalised graph shows timing and direction within each function, not direct comparability between their raw output scales. The infographics provide an accessible explanation of the same audited evidence.
