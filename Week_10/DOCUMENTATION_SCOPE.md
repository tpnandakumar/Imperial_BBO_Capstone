# Documentation Scope

Week 10 uses the same distinction applied elsewhere in the capstone repository. The raw submitted query vectors and platform returned objective values are primary evidence. Rankings, strategy labels, comparisons and narrative interpretation are derived from those verified observations.

The datasheet documents the Week 10 dataset and its provenance. The model card documents the human supervised, LLM assisted optimisation workflow used to interpret the accumulated evidence and support query selection.

Neither document claims access to the hidden mathematical functions or proof of a global optimum.
