# Post-BBO Black Box Resolution

This area contains resolution analyses applied to the completed official
thirteen-round challenge. They do not alter the portal submissions or results.
They use the fixed observation history to test what can and cannot be resolved
about the eight hidden functions.

The Delta prediction work predates this final BBO analysis. It developed as a
separate PIMF and PMOS investigation. PDHIS is the present name for that
existing mathematical line: Pisharam Delta Hierarchy and Influence States.
The work here applies it to all eight completed BBO sequences and tests its
predictive claims against chronology and randomised controls.

The PDHIS analysis separates calculation from interpretation. Delta levels are
measured finite differences. Influence states are candidate explanations that
require chronological validation and may be rejected when later evidence does
not support them.
