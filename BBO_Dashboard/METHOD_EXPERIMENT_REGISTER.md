# Imperial BBO Method and Experiment Register

This public register contains only methods supported by the weekly reflections, saved code or final evidence record. Course-module numbering and unverified course-linked experiments are deliberately excluded from the assessment-facing dashboard.

## Weekly development

1. Weeks 1 to 5 established function-specific exploration, exploitation and reassessment.
2. Week 6 added a separate validation experiment and more selective attention.
3. Weeks 7 and 8 used responsive redirection and selective continuation.
4. Week 9 strengthened transparency, provenance and repeatability documentation.
5. Week 10 analysed recurring regions and tuned the clustering comparison before Week 11.
6. Week 11 tested the regional strategy and applied PCA before Week 12.
7. Week 12 tested repetition, recovery, refinement and the PCA-supported decisions before the final query.
8. Week 13 evaluated the final outcomes through reward-based and sequential-decision concepts.

## Evidence timing

The repository distinguishes the week being analysed from the following submission. For example, Week 10 clustering analysis used Week 10 evidence to inform the Week 11 input. Week 11 PCA used the returned Week 11 evidence to inform Week 12.

## Evidence rule

A method is presented as decision-influencing only when the reflection or saved code connects the analysis to a later submitted coordinate. A course topic without BBO-specific evidence remains outside this public register until it is recovered or rebuilt as a clearly labelled retrospective experiment.
